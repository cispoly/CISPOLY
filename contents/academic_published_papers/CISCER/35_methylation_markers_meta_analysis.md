## Abstract

**Introduction:** DNA methylation markers have emerged as promising biomarkers for cervical cancer screening and triage. This systematic review and meta-analysis aimed to evaluate the diagnostic accuracy of DNA methylation markers for detecting cervical precancer (CIN2+) and cervical cancer.

**Methods:** A comprehensive search was conducted in PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, and Embase. Studies reporting diagnostic accuracy of DNA methylation markers (including PAX1, JAM3, FAM19A4, miR124, and others) for detecting CIN2+ or cervical cancer were included. Quality assessment was performed using QUADAS-2. Pooled sensitivity, specificity, and summary ROC curves were calculated.

**Conclusion:** DNA methylation markers, especially PAX1/JAM3 dual-gene methylation, demonstrate good diagnostic accuracy for detecting cervical precancer and cancer. These markers may serve as effective triage tools for hrHPV-positive women, potentially reducing unnecessary colposcopy referrals.
