5Risk Stratification & Value

  • High-risk flag: HPV16/18 + methylation+ → CIN2+ PPV up to 96.9% — prioritize colposcopy.
  • Low-risk triage: methylation-negative hrHPV carriers have markedly lower CIN2+ risk — fewer unnecessary referrals.
  • Uncertain cytology: ASC-US/LSIL cases can be objectively stratified by methylation status.
  • Objective + accessible: exfoliated-cell sampling is convenient for outpatient opportunistic screening.
  • Limit: preliminary, limited sample; needs larger prospective validation.
