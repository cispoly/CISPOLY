PAX1/JAM3 Methylation and HPV Viral Load in Women with Persistent HPV Infection

A 231-woman study of epigenetic and viral-load changes during HPV persistence — before any precancerous lesion
Li M, Zhao C, Zhang X, Li J, Zhao Y, Zhang W, Ren L, Wei L
Dept. of OB/GYN, Peking University People's Hospital · Cancers (Basel) 2024;16(7):1430 · DOI 10.3390/cancers16071430

1Why this study

  • Gap: during long HPV persistence there were no monitoring markers beyond repeated HPV tests & colposcopy — whether methylation or viral load (VL) shift before lesions was unknown.
  • Question: do PAX1/JAM3 methylation and HPV VL change with the duration of persistent HPV infection, in women without high-grade lesions (CIN2−)?
  • Why it matters: an early epigenetic signal could give cumulative evidence of persistence and flag faster-progressing women for closer follow-up.
  • Prior context: ~10–20% of HPV infections persist; only repeated HPV tests & colposcopy guided monitoring — no intermediate biomarker before CIN2+.

  • Setting: colposcopy clinic, Peking University People's Hospital; persistent HPV (same genotype ≥1 y) with histology excluding CIN2+.
  • Methylation: real-time PCR (ΔCt vs GAPDH); PAX1m+ = ΔCt ≤ 6.6, JAM3m+ = ΔCt ≤ 10.0.
  • Viral load: BMRT-HPV multiplex qPCR → log10 copies / 10,000 cells (TOP3-normalized).
  • Analysis: Mann–Whitney U, two-sided p<0.05; 266 screened → 231 analysed.

5Key results

  • Duration signal: JAM3m & PAX1m significantly higher when HPV persisted ≥3 y vs <3 y (p=0.007 / 0.025) — HPV VL was not associated with duration.
  • Co-methylation: PAX1 ↔ JAM3 strongly correlated (p<0.001) — usable as cumulative evidence of infection duration.
  • Vaginal lesions: ≥3-y persistence had more concurrent VaIN (p=0.031); high HPV VL (esp. non-16/18) marked concurrent cervical–vaginal disease.
  • Top persistent genotypes: HPV16 (24.6%), 52 (23.5%), 58 (15.5%); 81.8% had never been screened before.
  • Age: ≥3-y group older (48.9 vs 45.1 y, p=0.031); pathology (normal 67.5% / CIN1 32.5%) did not differ by duration.

6Clinical implication

  • Before lesions appear: rising PAX1/JAM3 in ≥3-y persistence offers an epigenetic read-out of cumulative HPV burden — earlier than repeat HPV testing alone.
  • VL ≠ duration: HPV VL did not track persistence here; it instead flagged concurrent vaginal lesions — a complementary, not redundant, marker.
  • Limitation: duration derived from retrospective screening history (often unclear); hidden CIN2 not fully excludable; single-centre.
  • Next steps: planned 1-y drug-intervention follow-up to exclude incident CIN2+, plus cervicovaginal microbiome analysis.

7Take-away

  • Mark persistence epigenetically: PAX1/JAM3 methylation rises once HPV lasts >3 y — potential cumulative marker before CIN2+ develops.
  • Two markers, two jobs: methylation = duration/burden read-out; HPV viral load = concurrent cervical–vaginal lesion flag (esp. non-16/18).
