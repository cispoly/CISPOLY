Clinical Significance
  • In non-16/18 hrHPV+ women, CISCER provides the widest risk spread (39.1% vs 0.9%) with the lowest referral rate — an efficient, objective triage.
  • Combining with LBC or HPV33/35 genotyping further raises positive risk (40–50%) while negatives stay near 0% — precise stratification.
