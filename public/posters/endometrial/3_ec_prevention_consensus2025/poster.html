1Background & Framework

  • Burden: EC most common gynecologic malignancy; #1 in Beijing/Shanghai/Guangzhou.
  • Model: cervical 3-level prevention mature; EC system needs unification.
  • Goal: reduce incidence · raise early diagnosis · improve survival & QoL.

  • Integrated 3-level prevention: risk control → early screening (methylation Se 87–94.5%) → MDT treatment & rehab — cutting EC incidence and mortality.
  • Methylation is an official screening option: two approved products (incl. CDO1/CELF4) with multi-route sampling — superior to traditional screening.
