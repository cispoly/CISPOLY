## Abstract

**Background:** High-risk human papillomavirus (hrHPV) testing has high sensitivity but low specificity for detecting high-grade cervical intraepithelial neoplasia (HSIL/CIN2+). PAX1/JAM3 methylation has been proposed as a triage biomarker. This meta-analysis aimed to evaluate the pooled diagnostic performance of PAX1/JAM3 methylation in hrHPV-positive women.

**Methods:** A systematic literature search was conducted in PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, and Cochrane Library. Studies evaluating PAX1/JAM3 methylation for detecting CIN2+ in hrHPV-positive women were included.
