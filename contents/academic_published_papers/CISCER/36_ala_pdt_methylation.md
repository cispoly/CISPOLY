## Abstract

**Background:** 5-aminolevulinic acid photodynamic therapy (ALA-PDT) is a minimally invasive treatment for cervical intraepithelial neoplasia (CIN) and other female lower reproductive tract intraepithelial lesions. However, predicting treatment response remains challenging. This study aimed to evaluate the efficacy of ALA-PDT and explore DNA methylation biomarkers for predicting treatment outcomes.

**Methods:** [Number] patients with CIN or vaginal intraepithelial neoplasia (VAIN) were treated with ALA-PDT. Clinical outcomes, including complete response rate, recurrence rate, and adverse events, were assessed. DNA methylation levels of [specific genes] were measured before and after treatment to explore their predictive value.

**Results:** The complete response rate of ALA-PDT was [value]%, with a recurrence rate of [value]% at [follow-up period]. Adverse events were mild and well-tolerated. [Specific gene] methylation levels were significantly associated with treatment response, suggesting its potential as a predictive biomarker.

**Conclusion:** ALA-PDT is an effective and safe treatment for female lower reproductive tract intraepithelial lesions. DNA methylation biomarkers may help predict treatment response and optimize patient selection. Further studies are needed to validate these findings.
