1Background & Objective

  • Problem: EC now #2 female genital malignancy in China; childbearing-age AUB needs non-invasive triage.
  • Objective: value of CDO1m/CELF4m on cervical cells for EC detection in childbearing-age AUB.

Clinical Significance
  • Best non-invasive option for young women with AUB: dual-gene methylation (Se 91.7%, AUC 0.90) protects fertility by avoiding unnecessary hysteroscopy.
  • TVS + methylation: sensitivity 95.8% — practical screening path with high NPV for childbearing-age AUB.
