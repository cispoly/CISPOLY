1Background & Objective

  • Access: self-sampling removes barriers (privacy, distance) — key for screening coverage.
  • Validate: is vaginal self-sample PAX1/JAM3 methylation as accurate as clinician sampling?

Clinical Significance
  • Vaginal self-sampling for PAX1/JAM3 methylation is feasible and accurate — high consistency with clinician sampling (r 0.83–0.86, κ 0.90) and good CIN2+/CIN3+ performance.
  • Self-sampling removes access barriers and supports broader, community-based cervical cancer screening.
