1Dual-Gene Biology & Advantage

PAX1
squamous-sensitive · early marker
JAM3
adenocarcinoma-sensitive · aggressive phenotype
  • Complementarity: PAX1 early & squamous; JAM3 aggressive & adenocarcinoma — joint detection boosts high-grade lesion detection.
  • Quantitative: ΔCt-based, objective reflection of epigenetic disorder — risk, type & stage assessment.

  • Self-sampling: home vaginal swab feasible — improves compliance & access.
  • Alternative: MSRE-qPCR panel (JAM3/PCDHGB7/SORCS1; PAX1/ZNF671/ASCL1) — no bisulfite, higher DNA recovery.
  • QC standard: 2025 five-society expert consensus — first national clinical standard for cervical DNA methylation.
  • Sample: cervical exfoliated cells — same brush & media as TCT/HPV, easy integration.
  • Readout rule: ΔCt PAX1 ≤ 6.6 or ΔCt JAM3 ≤ 10.0 → positive; lower ΔCt = higher methylation.
  • Clinical value: independent triage OR combined with HPV/TCT for screening.

Clinical Significance
  • PAX1/JAM3 dual-gene methylation is an objective, high-sensitivity & high-specificity molecular tool across the cervical lesion pathway — screening, hrHPV+/ASC-US triage, and CIN2+ post-treatment monitoring.
  • Future: AI + self-sampling + primary-care smart screening; post-op recurrence risk systems — full-course precision management of cervical cancer.
