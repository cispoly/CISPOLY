  • Precursor burden: high-grade CIN untreated → substantial progression risk to invasive cancer.
  • Conventional limits: HPV low specificity; TCT low sensitivity & operator-dependent — imperfect grading.
  • Methylation rationale: JAM3 (tight-junction molecule) promoter methylation is a key epigenetic event in carcinogenesis.
  • Aim: JAM3 methylation for HSIL detection, CIN grading and postoperative recurrence prediction.

  • Cases: 200 cervical biopsy cases requiring biopsy; single fixed sampler; histology-confirmed groups of 50 each.
  • Workup: HPV testing + liquid-based cytology (TCT) + JAM3 DNA methylation assay on pathological specimens.
