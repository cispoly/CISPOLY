1Background & Objective

  • Gap: no reliable screening test for endometrial cancer; invasive assessment is costly & fails in 11% postmenopausal.
  • Objective: validate epiHERA® (CDO1/CELF4 methylation of cervical scrapings) for EC detection & triage.

Clinical Significance
  • Non-invasive triage for endometrial cancer: cervical-scraping methylation (accuracy 97.3%, NPV 98.2%) reduces invasive endometrial assessment.
  • Early-disease signal: all false positives involved genital-tract neoplastic processes — may detect cancer before histologic change.
