1Background & Objective

  • Problem: hrHPV+ with normal cytology, ASC-US or LSIL — complex algorithms, excessive colposcopy referral.
  • Objective: test CISCER (PAX1/JAM3 methylation) for managing minimally abnormal results.

Clinical Significance
  • CISCER resolves the minimally abnormal management dilemma — excellent CIN3+ detection (Se 74.9%, Sp 89.1%, AUC 0.820).
  • 15× risk separation (40.5% vs 2.7%) enables safe deferral of CISCER(−) women — substantially fewer direct colposcopy referrals.
