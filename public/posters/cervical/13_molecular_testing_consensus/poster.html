1Consensus Position on Methylation

  • 2021 WHO (2nd ed.): DNA methylation — future molecular technology for HPV-based screening.
  • 2024 Methylation Consensus: methylation enters cervical cancer screening clinical pathway.
  • Blue Book: methylation as promising screening & triage tool.
  • 2025 Consensus: HPV & DNA methylation recommended as molecular screening (2A).
