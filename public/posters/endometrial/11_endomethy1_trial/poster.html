1Background & Objective

  • Problem: no guideline-recommended biomarkers for EC screening; biopsy invasive & costly.
  • Objective: develop & validate hypermethylated-gene panel on cervical cytology (EndoMethy-I).

Clinical Significance
  • Reliable, safe EC detection: cervical cytology CDO1/CELF4/NEFM methylation (Se 94.6%, Sp 92.8%) — superior to other non-invasive parameters.
  • Decision-tree triage: methylation-based strategies cut hysteroscopy to ~25% with minimal missed EC — a scalable screening workflow.
