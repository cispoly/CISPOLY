**The EUROGIN Congress's Unique Focus:**

Centered on HPV and cervical cancer, covering the entire chain from basic research to clinical practice and public health.

Emphasizes technology innovation and resource equity, promoting screening technology accessibility and vaccine availability.

Focuses on global collaboration, particularly addressing the needs of low- and middle-income countries, supporting the global goal of eliminating cervical cancer.

So how do we participate in such international conferences?

![](./images/contents/中国甲基化技术革新宫颈癌筛查从实验室到全球临床实践的新范式-img-02.jpg)

The ESGO meeting (European Society of Gynaecological Oncology) is Europe's leading gynecologic cancer research organization, dedicated to improving the health and well-being of gynecologic cancer patients through prevention, excellence in care, high-quality research, and education.

For such international conferences, especially to present as an oral report, submitters need to complete their abstracts. Submission and conference opening dates should be identified early on the official website. International conferences are a larger global stage, and in the coming years, more Chinese methylation research is expected to appear at international conferences like ESGO.

CISPOLY's methylation technology has participated in numerous well-known international conferences in recent years, including EUROGIN, ESGO, International Conference on Gynecology and Obstetrics, and others.

![](./images/contents/中国甲基化技术革新宫颈癌筛查从实验室到全球临床实践的新范式-img-03.jpg)

At the 2024 25th European Society of Gynaecological Oncology (ESGO) Congress, CISPOLY and domestic physician collaborators presented the CISENDO® endometrial cancer (CDO1 and CELF4) methylation non-invasive screening plan as an oral presentation for the first time at ESGO, receiving high praise from Nadeem R. Abu-Rustum, Chair of the endometrial cancer session from Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, USA.

**About the CISENDO® Endometrial Cancer Methylation Detection Kit**

The National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) approved the product registration application for the "Human CDO1 and CELF4 Gene Methylation Detection Kit (PCR-Fluorescent Probe Method)" (Trade name: CISENDO® Endometrial Cancer Methylation Detection Kit) (Registration No.: 20243402610) — the first approved non-invasive endometrial cancer detection product. Using cervical-collected specimens combined with _CDO1_ and _CELF4_ gene methylation detection technology, it effectively reduces the risks and operational difficulty of traditional intrauterine sampling and demonstrates excellent detection performance, with a sensitivity of 94.51% and specificity of 94.16%. It avoids the risks associated with intrauterine sampling and is suitable for widespread adoption in outpatient and primary healthcare settings. Additionally, this technology enables non-invasive methylation assessment prior to hysteroscopy, significantly reducing the risk of physical injury to patients and providing a safer, more convenient solution for early endometrial cancer screening.
