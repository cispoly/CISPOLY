4Testing Requirements

qPCR
qPCR: 1–few markers; gene-amplification lab rules.
dPCR
dPCR: trace/low-frequency absolute quantitation.
NGS
NGS: hundreds–whole genome; GB/T 30989.
MS
MALDI-TOF MS: multi-CpG level analysis per kit.
  • Extraction: column/magnetic bead; separate cfDNA & cell zones; tumor enrichment if <20% cells; purity OD260/280 1.8–2.0, ≥10 ng/μL.
  • Conversion: bisulfite or enzymatic; check efficiency & recovery.
  • Detection: SOP per method; cfDNA fragment profile checked for gDNA contamination.

Clinical Significance
  • Standardized laboratory pathway: pre-test → testing → post-test requirements with three-level QC ensure reliable tumor methylation results.
  • Practice-ready: 7 specimen types, 4 platforms, evidence-graded reporting — basis for methylation assays entering screening/triage workflows.
