Clinical Significance
  • Self-sampling is a reliable, non-invasive EC triage: CDO1/CELF4 methylation AUC 0.930 — equivalent to physician sampling (0.896).
  • Access & early detection: home-based collection can be integrated into diagnostic workflows before hysteroscopy.
