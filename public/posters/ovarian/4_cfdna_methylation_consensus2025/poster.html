  • Screening: Septin9 Se 48.2% (advanced adenoma 11.2%) — insufficient for precancer; MCED models (Galleri Se 51.5/Sp 99.5; PanSeer 95.7%).
  • MRD: Septin9/BCAN/BCAT1/IKZF1/VAV3 — postoperative (+) vs (−) 17.5× recurrence risk.
  • dPCR edge: 4-marker dPCR for NSCLC reaches Se 90% — outperforms regular PCR.
  • Quality link: interpretation only after QC pass; lab provides clinical consultation.
  • Future: AI/ML multi-marker models · standardization · population heterogeneity research.
  • Lab challenge: cfDNA low concentration · short half-life · high background — standardized QC essential.
  • Positioning: adjunct diagnosis first; screening & MRD advancing with MCED & multi-marker AI models.
