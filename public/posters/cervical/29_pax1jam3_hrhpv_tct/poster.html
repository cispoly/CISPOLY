5Risk Stratification & Significance

  • Independent association: HR-HPV, non-NILM TCT and PAX1/JAM3 methylation each independently link to high-grade lesions.
  • Objective add-on: methylation quantifies host transformation — strengthens risk stratification beyond HPV/TCT alone.
  • Decision support: positive methylation flags high-risk women for colposcopy despite negative cytology.
  • Next steps: larger prospective studies to validate utility and cost-effectiveness in routine screening.
