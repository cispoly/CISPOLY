1Background & Objective

  • Limits of hrHPV/LBC: hrHPV low specificity; LBC sensitivity 50–80% & subjective ASC-US reads.
  • Objective: evaluate JAM3/PAX1 methylation vs LBC for HSIL — alone or combined.

  • Methylation stratifies HSIL independently of LBC — a cytology-free triage path for hrHPV+ women.
  • High-NPV tree rules (ΔCtJ >10.13 → 100% non-HSIL) enable safe deferral — fewer colposcopies, earlier treatment.
