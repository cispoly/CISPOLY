# Self-Sampling for CDO1/CELF4 Methylation Accurately Identifies Endometrial Cancer in a Resource-Limited Region: A Prospective Study From Gansu

Feixue Xu, Suyue Yin, Min Wei, Jia Wu, Panpan Yang, Jiao Yang, Yuqi Xin, Pei Liu, Yishan Wang, Xingyu Liu, Kaiya Zhao, Aihong Yang, Yu-Ligh Liou

Citation: Int J Cancer. 2026 Oct 30. Online ahead of print. doi: 10.1002/ijc.70776

## 摘要

在医疗资源有限地区，子宫内膜癌（EC）的及时诊断与风险分层仍面临困难。本项前瞻性诊断准确性与可行性研究评估了自采宫颈样本联合CDO1/CELF4甲基化检测（CISENDO），能否在中国西北部甘肃省有症状女性中识别经组织病理学确诊的EC。在302名接受筛选的女性中，154名同时具备自采样本和组织病理学诊断的参与者构成主要分析队列，其中35例为EC，119例为非EC；另有35名同时具备自采与医生采集样本的参与者纳入探索性配对分析。采用受试者工作特征曲线分析和Logistic回归评估诊断效能，并通过相关性、Bland–Altman分析及分类一致性评价配对采样结果。

在自采样本中，采用厂家规定阈值的CISENDO对EC具有较好的区分能力（AUC为0.834）。CDO1与CELF4的Bland–Altman平均差值分别为−1.05 ΔCt和−1.73 ΔCt。35对样本中，31对CISENDO分类结果一致（88.6%），阳性一致率为84.6%，阴性一致率为90.9%，Cohen's κ为0.76。调整后，CISENDO阳性仍为EC最强的独立预测因子（调整后OR为85.08，95% CI为12.21–592.71，p < 0.001）。子宫内膜厚度的区分能力相对有限（AUC为0.646），与CISENDO联合未改善诊断效能。

自采样CISENDO检测对经组织病理学确诊的EC表现出较好的区分能力，可能为医疗服务不足地区的有症状女性提供可行的分子分流和风险分层方法。这一方式可支持分散采样，并减少对即时专科评估的依赖，尤其适用于地域分散、医疗资源有限的人群。

**关键词：** 子宫内膜癌；自采样；CDO1甲基化；CELF4甲基化；医疗资源有限地区。

## Abstract

Timely diagnosis and risk stratification of endometrial cancer (EC) remain difficult in resource-limited regions. This prospective diagnostic accuracy and feasibility study evaluated whether self-collected cervical specimens combined with CDO1/CELF4 methylation testing, defined as the CISENDO assay, could identify histologically confirmed EC in symptomatic women from Gansu, northwest China. Of 302 screened women, 154 participants with self-collected specimens and histopathological diagnoses formed the primary analytic cohort; 35 had EC and 119 had non-EC diagnoses. An exploratory paired subset included 35 participants with both self- and physician-collected specimens. Diagnostic performance was assessed using receiver operating characteristic analysis and logistic regression, while paired sampling was evaluated using correlation, Bland-Altman analysis, and classification concordance. In self-collected specimens, CISENDO showed strong discrimination for EC at manufacturer-defined cutoffs (AUC 0.834). Bland-Altman mean differences were -1.05 ΔCt for CDO1 and -1.73 ΔCt for CELF4. CISENDO classifications were concordant in 31/35 paired specimens (88.6%), with positive agreement of 84.6%, negative agreement of 90.9%, and Cohen's κ of 0.76. CISENDO positivity remained the strongest independent predictor of EC after adjustment (adjusted OR 85.08; 95% CI 12.21-592.71; p < 0.001). Endometrial thickness showed modest discrimination (AUC 0.646) and did not improve performance when combined with CISENDO. Self-collected CISENDO testing showed strong discrimination for histologically confirmed EC and may provide a feasible molecular triage and risk-stratification approach for symptomatic women in underserved settings. This approach could support decentralized sample collection and reduce dependence on immediate specialist-based evaluation, particularly in geographically dispersed, resource-constrained populations.

**Keywords:** CDO1 methylation; CELF4 methylation; endometrial cancer; resource-limited region; self-sampling.

## 结论

自采样CDO1/CELF4甲基化检测（CISENDO）在甘肃有症状女性中显示出识别子宫内膜癌的诊断区分能力，为医疗资源有限地区的分子分流与风险分层提供了前瞻性证据。35例配对样本的结果支持自采样的可行性；研究结论适用于本研究的有症状就诊人群。
