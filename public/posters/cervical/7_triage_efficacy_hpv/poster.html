1Background & Objective

  • Challenge: hrHPV+ is mostly transient; cytology triage is subjective with lower sensitivity.
  • Objective: real-world evaluation of CISCER® PAX1/JAM3 methylation for triaging hrHPV+ women across genotypes & ages.

Clinical Significance
  • For hrHPV+ women, PAX1/JAM3 methylation (CISCER®) significantly outperforms cytology across HPV genotypes and age groups — 92.6% sensitivity, 95.7% specificity, AUC 0.941, and 100% cancer detection.
  • Age-stratified value: 100% sensitivity in ≥50 y, 100% specificity in <30 y — a single test that reduces both missed disease and overtreatment.
