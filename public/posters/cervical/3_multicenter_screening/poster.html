1Background & Objective

  • Problem: hrHPV screening is sensitive but low-specificity; cytology triage is subjective & poorly reproducible.
  • Objective: multicenter prospective validation of PAX1m/JAM3m vs TCT & hrHPV for CIN2+/CIN3+.

Clinical Significance
  • PAX1m/JAM3m shows excellent accuracy for CIN2+ and CIN3+ in a multicenter prospective cohort — superior to TCT & hrHPV (AUC 0.872 vs 0.580/0.503).
  • As hrHPV reflex triage, methylation improves specificity and reduces unnecessary colposcopy — a practical screening upgrade.
